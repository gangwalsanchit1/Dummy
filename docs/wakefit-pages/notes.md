# Wakefit product pages: finishes, sizes, prices

Read from wakefit.co in Chrome on 2026-11-03, during the Diwali sale (ends 10 Nov), so prices will change.

- **Price** is the sale price on the page, including taxes. MRP is in brackets.
- **W x D x H** is in cm, taken from each page's specification section. Where the page's own figures are muddled, the raw text is quoted under the table.
- The finish list on each product covers the configuration linked here (size, storage, doors and so on). The colour picker only offers finishes that exist for that configuration.
- Photos: `<product>/<finish>.jpg` is the first gallery image for that finish, resized to 800 px wide. `<finish>-swatch.jpg` is the swatch tile from the variant picker (256 px).
