## Living in the room (a student's view)

**Each student has their own:** bed (with storage under it), study table, chair, wardrobe, a bedside socket + USB + reading-light switch within reach of the pillow, and 2 sockets + USB + data at the desk.

**Checked in the model for every student:** they get to their bed without climbing over the other bed; their chair has its own floor space; their wardrobe doors open without hitting anything; nothing tall blocks a window; the switchboard by the door is reachable; and a walking path reaches all of it.
