## How Bella Vita 2 provides the same, within the drawn building

Nothing here changes walls, rooms, toilets, slabs or structure. All positions are ASSUMED proposals and are shown in the viewer and on the E and P sheets.

| Amenity at Bella Vita | Bella Vita 2 proposal | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Single bed + wardrobe | Wakefit furniture (sizes from the brief) in every room. A double (queen) only where it fits with clearances and paths; otherwise a single. See `docs/furniture-fit.md`. | All rooms |
| Air conditioning | AC unit and 20 A socket in every room, outdoor units on brackets or terraces | All rooms |
| CCTV | 9 cameras: stilt, gate, terrace and every lobby | Common areas |
| RFID access | RFID reader and magnetic lock at the ground-floor lobby entry. Battery RFID locks on every room door (no wiring). | GF entry, all room doors |
| Wi-Fi | Ceiling access point in every lobby, plus an outdoor access point for the terrace lounge | Lobbies, terrace |
| Power backup | Wall-mounted inverter and lithium battery in the ground-floor grey niche. It backs up common lights, CCTV, Wi-Fi, RFID and the lift's automatic rescue device. | GF niche |
| RO drinking water | RO purifier at the pantry, and a floor water dispenser with a socket in the F1–F3 lobbies. **Floor 4's lobby is too small for one** without blocking the ROOM-3 or ROOM-2 door. | GF pantry, F1–F3 lobbies |
| Meals | The drawn pantry (sink, RO, fridge and microwave sockets). Cooking and dining are an operator decision; nothing is added. | GF pantry |
| Professional laundry | **Terrace laundry corner:** 2 washing machines on the rear parapet with taps and 16 A sockets, plus a kerbed floor trap draining to the rear waste stack through a parapet sleeve. The same corner also holds the **cleaner's sink** that IS 1172 Table 11 asks for. | Terrace, rear |
| Study spaces, library, gaming zone, community lounge | **Terrace lounge**, open-air, using movable furniture only: lights, weatherproof sockets and an outdoor Wi-Fi access point. Rooms also have desks with desk power and data. | Terrace |
| Electricity billing (common PG practice) | Prepaid energy sub-meter per room in each floor distribution board | Floor DBs |
| Name board | Signage light under the F1 slab edge at the front | Front |

### Not included

- **Indoor common room, library or dining room:** there is no drawn space for one. The stilt is parking, so I have not proposed using it.
- **Terrace shade structures:** a pergola or shed would be structure. Umbrellas and movable furniture only.
