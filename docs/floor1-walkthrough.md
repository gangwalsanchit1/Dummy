# Floor 1: walking through each room as the students (feet and inches)

Family, 03.11.2026: *"Imagine you're person A for each room, go to each room from the lobby and see it from their perspective, then do the same for person B."* and *"Human space should be walkable. Your roommate uses the WC, so you don't want your bed right next to the bathroom."*

The layouts below were designed by hand from that walk-through (the automatic search kept producing layouts that were legal but not liveable), then audited by `tools/check_layout.py`: every rule, every room PASS (`docs/layout-audit.md`). Floors 2 and 3 are identical.

**Rules that came out of the walk-through:**
- **Toilet:** no bed within 2'-11⅜" of a toilet door, and no bed head on a toilet wall, so night-time flushing does not wake the roommate.
- **Walking space:** a clear 2'-0" walkway everywhere a person walks, and a wider route from the entry to the toilet.
- **Doors:** chairs and wardrobe doors are never inside a door swing.
- **Windows:** nothing tall stands in front of a window.
- **Even look:** wardrobes and desks sit flush on plain walls (never on or proud of a column), and matching pairs line up or face each other.
- **Beds:** you climb in at the pillow end or the middle, so the last 1'-0" at the foot needs no access space.

## ROOM-1 (2 students) and ROOM-2 (2 students, mirror image)

1. **Entering from the lobby:** the door opens inwards against the side wall. Straight ahead, a clear 3'-10⅞" route leads to the toilet door.
2. **Wardrobes:** on the left in ROOM-1, on the right in ROOM-2. Both Twills sit side by side on the side wall, between the entry and the toilet. Bags, clothes and towels are sorted on the way in.
3. **The two beds:** one bed runs along the top wall, the other along the front wall. Both heads are on the outer side wall, the far side of the room from the toilet. The beds face each other across the room.
   - **Distance from the toilet door:** 3'-4⅜" for the nearer bed, 4'-4½" for the farther one in ROOM-1, and 4'-6⅜" for the farther one in ROOM-2. Neither bed has its head on a toilet wall.
4. **Side tables:** each student has their own Tinker at the pillow end, with their own bedside socket, USB and reading-light switch above it.
5. **Desks:** each student has their own Cabrera desk and chair. One is on the outer side wall and the other on the toilet-side wall, so the two desks face each other across the room.
   - They are **offset by 11¾"**. A column (C11 in ROOM-1, C9 in ROOM-2) and the end of the toilet wall make an exact mirror impossible.
   - No chair is in a door swing.
6. **Window and AC:** the front bed runs along the front window, so that window is opened over the bed. The bed is below the assumed 2'-11⅜" sill. The AC sits above that window and throws its air across the room, away from the bed.

## ROOM-3 (2 students)

1. **Entering from the lobby:** the door, in the corner by the moved wall, opens inwards. On your left, by the entry, both Twills stand side by side on the moved wall.
2. **Walkway:** a straight 2'-3⅛" walkway runs along the right side of the room from the entry to the toilet door. Neither student crosses the other's bed or chair to reach the toilet.
3. **Beds:** both beds have their heads on the outer side wall, with **one shared Tinker** between them (family choice) and a bedside socket for each student on either side of it.
   - **Distance from the toilet door:** 3'-8⅛" for the nearer bed, 9'-1" for the other.
   - Column C1 sits behind one headboard. Both beds are set 3⅛" off the wall so the headboards line up.
4. **Study nook:** both Cabrera desks sit side by side in the window alcove, so both students get daylight, and a clear band runs in front of the alcove. The AC above the alcove window cools the study nook and is 9'-10⅞" from the nearer bed.

## ROOM-4 (1 student)

1. **Entering from the lobby:** you come down the entry passage, and the toilet is at its end.
2. **Wardrobe and desk:** turning right into the room, the Twill comes first on the study wall, then the Cabrera desk, both on the study wall. Column C13 takes the first 11¾" of that wall.
3. **Bed:** the Orion queen has its head on the outer side wall, 4'-1⅞" from the toilet door.
4. **AC:** moved to the left of the window, over the study area, so it no longer blows at the pillow end. Its pipe run to the toilet window also became shorter.
