# Full luxury theme: Plan A and Plan B with TV and sofa (feet and inches)

Plans: `docs/plan-a-luxury-plan.png`, `docs/plan-b-luxury-plan.png`. Audits: `docs/plan-a-luxury-audit.md`, `docs/plan-b-luxury-audit.md` (every rule, every room PASS). Products and quantities: `docs/wakefit-products.md`. The saved Plan A and the plain Plan B are unchanged.

**Luxury items used**
- **Sofa:** Wakefit **Flipper 2-seater sofa cum bed**, 116.8 × 76.2 × 71.2 cm (3'-10" × 2'-6"). It is Wakefit's most compact sofa and opens into a 6' × 4' guest bed. The size comes from Flipkart and Amazon.in listings.
- **TV:** a wall-mounted TV of any brand (Wakefit does not sell TVs), 42" or 40". It takes no floor space, with its bottom edge about 3'-7" above the floor.
  - Wakefit's wall-mounted Murphy TV unit (110 cm wide) does not fit any room without blocking a walkway or a desk, so the TVs go on wall brackets.

**Rules added**
- **Sofa:** it is flush on a plain wall, its 1'-5¾" legroom stays out of every door swing, and every walkway stays 2'-0".
- **TV:**
  - it faces the place you would actually watch from;
  - it is never over a window or above a bed head;
  - it never clashes with anything tall;
  - the viewing distance is reported (a 42" TV is most comfortable from about 5'-3" to 8'-10").

## Why there is no sofa in the other rooms

- **Twin rooms (ROOM-1, ROOM-2, ROOM-3 in Plan A):** every wall already holds a student's bed, desk or wardrobe. A sofa would cost one student their desk or wardrobe, or block the walkway to the toilet.
- **ROOM-4:** the family rule puts the desk and wardrobe on the study wall, which then has no length left (5'-3" beside column C13). The queen takes the rest of the room.
  - Under the window, the sofa would sit in front of the wardrobe doors or in the desk-chair space.
  - Against the stair wall, its legroom would run into the desk.
- **Plan B's ROOM-3** has a sofa because it is a single, and the walkway runs down the other side of the room.

## Notes

- **Viewing distance in ROOM-1 and ROOM-2:** the far bed is about 10'-2" from the 42" TV. A 50" TV would suit that bed better, and it fits the same wall.
- **TV points:** each TV needs a socket and a data or cable point behind it. They will be added to the electrical layout once you choose a plan.
- **Luxury touches that need no floor space** (already in the electrical proposal, or for fit-out):
  - LED cove lights over the windows;
  - a reading-light switch at every pillow;
  - full-length mirrors;
  - blackout curtains.

## Father's hotel layout, moved to ROOM-3 (03.11.2026)

**Why not ROOM-4:** his wish list for ROOM-4 was:
- the bed in the middle with its headboard opposite the door;
- the sofa at the far end facing a TV, with the study table under the TV;
- a bigger (Gingham) wardrobe in front of the bed.

ROOM-4 (11'-2" × 9'-2") is about 2 ft too small for that. Every arrangement was checked: a queen with space on both sides, a Gingham and a study table do not fit together there even without the sofa. The family chose to move the layout to ROOM-3 (154 sq ft); ROOM-4 keeps its design.

**ROOM-3 now (Plan B luxury)**
- **Bed:** the Orion queen is in the middle of the room, with its headboard on the wall opposite the entry door and a Tinker on each side. Both long sides are clear, and it is 3'-0¼" from the toilet door.
- **Lounge nook (the far end):** the Wakefit Flipper sofa is on one side of the window alcove, facing a 32" TV on the opposite wall, 3'-10⅜" away (the right size of TV for that distance).
- **Study table:** a Cabrera under the window, right beside the TV.
  - It cannot go directly under the TV: in the 5'-3" alcove its chair would hit the sofa.
  - It is the Cabrera rather than the Sage, because the Sage's hutch would block the window.
- **Wardrobe:** the Gingham (the bigger wardrobe, with mirror) is on the wall facing the bed.
- **One exception, approved by the family:** the walkway beside the foot of the bed is 1'-11¼" (rule 2'-0").
