# Plan B (father's scenario): luxury singles in ROOM-3 and ROOM-4, no wall or door changes

The plan is `docs/scenario-dad-plan.png`, the audit is `docs/dad-luxury-singles-audit.md` (every rule, every room PASS), and the product list is `docs/wakefit-products.md`. Floors 2 and 3 are identical. The saved Plan A is unchanged.

## What Plan B is

- **No wall or door changes.** It uses the architect's AFTER O.C. plan exactly as drawn. The ROOM-3/ROOM-2 wall and both doors stay where the architect put them, so nothing needs re-drawing or a beam check.
- **ROOM-1 and ROOM-2 (front): two students each.** Each student has their own Centaur single bed, Tinker, Twill wardrobe, Cabrera study table and chair.
- **ROOM-3 and ROOM-4: luxury singles.** Each has an Orion queen bed, a Tinker, a Twill wardrobe and the Sage study table with hutch.
  - **Why the Twill, not the Gingham:** the Gingham (mirror) would fit ROOM-3, but ROOM-4's study wall is only 5'-3" beside column C13, so it takes the Twill. Both luxury rooms get the Twill so they match.

## Walking through Plan B

**ROOM-3 (luxury single)**
- **Entering:** the door opens inwards. A clear walkway, more than 4'-0" wide, runs along the right side to the toilet.
- **Bed:** the queen's long side is against the outer side wall and its head is on the ROOM-2 wall, 5'-5¾" from the toilet door. The Tinker is on the open side at the pillow end.
- **Study:** the Sage desk (with hutch) is in the window alcove, a study nook with daylight.
- **Wardrobe:** the Twill is on the right wall beside the toilet, a dressing spot on the way to the bathroom.

**ROOM-4 (luxury single)**
- **Entering:** down the passage, with the toilet at its end. Turning into the room, the Sage desk comes first on the study wall, then the Twill towards the window.
- **Bed:** the queen's long side is on the outer side wall and its head is on the stair wall, 6'-2⅝" from the toilet door. The Tinker sits on the open side at the head (family rule for ROOM-4).
- **AC:** left of the window, over the study area, away from the pillow.

**ROOM-1 and ROOM-2 (two students each)**
- They have the same arrangement as Plan A: wardrobes by the entry, beds with their heads on the outer wall away from the toilet, and desks facing each other.
- In Plan B, ROOM-2 keeps its original door and size. Its two Twills stand side by side on the wall by the entry.
