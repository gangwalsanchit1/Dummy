Floor 4 total: 2 Orion, 2 Centaur, 5 Tinker, 2 Gingham, 2 Twill, 4 Cabrera, 4 study chairs; 4 students. Whole building: `docs/wakefit-products.md`.

Why these choices:
- **Twill, not Gingham, in the front rooms:** in ROOM-1 the door swings over the alcove, and only the Twill (1'-11⅝") fits clear of it. ROOM-2 takes the same Twill so the two rooms match.
- **Wardrobe position:** the picture shows the wardrobe beside the bed head. There is no room there for both the Tinker and a wardrobe before the door swing (ROOM-2) or column C7 (ROOM-1), so the wardrobe goes in the alcove instead (the architect's 0.55 m strip, as on floors 1–3).

Open points:
- **Front terrace has no door on the architect's drawing** (only windows onto it), so the chairs in the picture could not be reached. It is shown with planters. If the family wants to sit there, the architect would have to add a door.
- **Columns on floor 4** are drawn as continuing from floor 3; the structural drawing covers the stilt and three floors only. The structural engineer must confirm the floor 4 columns before this layout is relied on.
- The suite is laid out for two people, as the picture shows two queen beds; say if it is meant for one.
